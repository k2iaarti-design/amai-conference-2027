About the Conference

India’s Alkali Industry: Entering a New Era of Growth

Building on the success of previous AMAI conferences, the
AMAI Annual Alkali Industry Conference & Exhibition 2027 will bring together Indian and international producers, global energy experts, technology licensors, equipment manufacturers and suppliers, safety professionals, logistics specialists, policymakers, regulators, and sustainability leaders on a common platform to explore emerging opportunities and address key challenges.
